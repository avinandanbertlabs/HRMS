# Organization hierarchy and reporting model

Company → optional Business Unit/Division → Department → Team → Employees. Job titles such as Department Head, Team Lead and Manager are configurable designations, not hard-coded hierarchy levels. Support direct reports to CEO, secondary functional reporting and acting managers.

Rules: no self-reporting or circular primary reporting; one active primary manager at a time; effective start/end dates; preserve history; acting manager permissions must be explicitly delegated and time-bound; changes audited. Manager access derives from reporting scope and explicit grants, not title. Decide whether in-flight approvals remain with the original approver or are rerouted when managers change.
